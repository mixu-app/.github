# Trademark policy

Status: **Decided** · Applies to every mixu-app repository

"Mixu", "谜序", the Mixu logo and any confusingly similar name or mark are
product identity of the project owner. Open-source licenses used in these
repositories (Apache-2.0, MPL-2.0, CC-BY-4.0, CC0-1.0) grant **no** trademark
or brand rights. Apache-2.0 §6 and MPL-2.0 §2.3 say this explicitly.

## What you may do without asking

- State truthfully that your project uses or is derived from Mixu source.
- Keep the attribution required by the license, including the project name in a
  copyright notice.
- Write about Mixu in articles, talks and comparisons.

## What needs permission

- Using "Mixu"/"谜序", the logo, or a confusingly similar mark as the name of
  your app, product, service, package, or repository.
- Publishing a modified build under the Mixu name, or implying it is official,
  endorsed, or produced by the project owner.
- Using the marks in a domain name, app-store listing, or paid advertising.

## Forks

A fork is welcome and lawful. Rename it, replace the logo and the app icon, and
change the application id, so a user cannot mistake it for the official app. Do
not present a fork as "Mixu" or "谜序", and do not reuse the brand assets
distributed with the official build.

## Reporting misuse

Open an issue in this repository, or use a private channel if the report involves
a counterfeit build or a security risk. We prefer a correction over a dispute.

## Note

This policy describes our practice; it is not legal advice, and it does not
limit any right granted by applicable law or by the open-source licenses
themselves.
